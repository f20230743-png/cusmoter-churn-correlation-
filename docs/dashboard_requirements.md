# Dashboard Requirements

## Audience

E-commerce leadership, marketplace operations, seller performance teams, and data analyst hiring reviewers.

## Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Overview
2. Revenue and Product Categories
3. Delivery SLA and Customer Experience
4. Seller Performance
5. Customer Geography and Repeat Behaviour

## KPIs

- Total orders
- Total revenue
- Average order value
- Late delivery rate
- Average review score
- Repeat customer rate
- Revenue by category
- Seller tier performance
- State-level customer value

## Required Slicers

- Product category
- Customer state
- Seller state
- Seller tier
- Late delivery flag
- Review score group

## Acceptance Criteria

- Revenue totals reconcile to enriched order data.
- Late delivery rate uses delivered orders as denominator.
- Seller tier is reproducible from SLA and review metrics.
- Dashboard includes commercial, operational, and customer experience views.
