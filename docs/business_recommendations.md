# Business Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The analysis links revenue, delivery performance, seller quality, and customer review outcomes. The goal is to help marketplace teams prioritise seller improvement actions and category growth opportunities.

## Recommended Actions

- Prioritise high-revenue categories with strong review scores for promotional growth.
- Investigate categories with high revenue but weak delivery or review performance.
- Monitor seller tiers and intervene with sellers creating repeated SLA misses.
- Use late delivery as a customer experience risk indicator because delayed orders tend to reduce review quality.
- Build state-level campaigns where order value and repeat behaviour are strongest.

## Measurement Plan

- Track late delivery rate weekly.
- Track seller tier movement monthly.
- Monitor review score after seller interventions.
- Compare category revenue before and after promotional campaigns.
